Origination order & three columns

Side, total, moneyline in that order — and fair / market / difference as three separate, recomputable columns.

Origination order — side, then total, then moneyline

The order is not a layout preference. It fixes what is fitted, what may reuse what, and what is derived. Originating the three markets independently produces the familiar inconsistency where a team is a 60% cover at −2.5 and a 45% moneyline favourite.

The consistency test passes at machine precision. Across 6,073 games the side partition sums to one with a worst error of 1.1e-16 and the moneyline partition with 1.1e-16. Because both are evaluations of the same empirical residual distribution rather than separate fits, the moneyline cannot disagree with the side at handicap zero. This is internal consistency only — it says nothing about calibration against outcomes, which needs the validation gates and an authenticated price.

What a difference is not. A fair line is a model estimate and a market line from this archive is an undated community value. The difference is arithmetic over the two; it is not an edge, because an edge requires an authenticated executable price.
