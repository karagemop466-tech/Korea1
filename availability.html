How to read this page

  • First-listed is the player the club prints first in a depth-chart slot. 2nd / 3rd / 4th string is the club’s printed order for the same slot. Clubs describe these charts as unofficial; the order is not a confirmed game-day lineup.
  • Next listed appears only when the first-listed player is designated Out or Doubtful. It is the next player in the slot who is not himself Out or Doubtful.
  • The page does not estimate how many points an absence is worth. That is open question OQ-07; the options are on the decision board (DB-05, DB-06).
  • Players on the injury report are matched to depth charts by name within the same club. Unmatched names are listed in their own table, never guessed.
  • Parallel sessions published a source-by-source audit of the official injury pages at Injury source audit and a hand-checked sample at Lineup sample. Those pages describe two clubs; this page is the all-club capture.
