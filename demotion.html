Demotion gate

Six fail-closed triggers that send a live strategy back to paper — armed, evaluated, and honest about an empty live record.

What this page is. Requirement 8 of the 2026-11-14 brief: a demotion gate that sends a live strategy back to paper. Every existing gate asked whether a strategy may be funded; this one asks whether it must be stopped, and it answers with six fail-closed triggers, each naming the statistic that enforces it.

1 · The four states

  • PAPER — not funded. Evidence may be accumulating; nothing is staked.
  • LIVE — funded, with a live record under observation.
  • DEMOTED — was LIVE, sent back to PAPER by this gate. The trigger and its evidence are kept on the row, so a demotion is auditable and reversible on new evidence.
  • RETIRED — out of the competition. Only the owner can retire a strategy; the gate cannot.

4 · Promotion is not the mirror image

Nothing here promotes a strategy. Moving PAPER → LIVE requires all of the following, and the gate prints the checklist rather than executing it:

  1. all eight validation gates pass (Validation gates);
  2. `signal_verdict = SIGNAL_PRESENT` on the backtest (Signal vs staking);
  3. a positive staking interval that survives the 5-cent adverse price stress;
  4. a completed prospective window with settled official results (Verified results feed);
  5. an owner decision recorded in DECISIONS.md — funding is a decision with a cost, and the rules cannot make it.

Today: no strategy meets item 1 (gate G1 — authenticated executable prices — fails for every candidate), so no strategy can be promoted, and none is LIVE, so none can be demoted. That is the correct outcome for an evidence set with no verified prices, and the gate says it plainly instead of printing a green tick.
