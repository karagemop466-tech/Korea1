Important definitions

  • Overround is the sum of implied probabilities minus one. Hold is sportsbook revenue divided by handle; they are not interchangeable.
  • MAE compares absolute point errors. Brier score evaluates probability calibration; neither is betting ROI.
  • CLV compares entry and closing prices at the same book, line and terms. It requires real timestamps.
  • Holm p controls family-wise error under arbitrary dependence. Deflated Sharpe is an approximation sensitive to the trial count and return distribution.
  • Verified means checked against the cited authoritative source. Hash-verified means only that downloaded bytes match a pin.
