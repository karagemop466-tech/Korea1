Evidence boundary

  • Opening numbers: nflverse/nfldata initial_lines.csv — the 2021 season only, book WSGT, spread and total lines only. The archive carries no moneyline openers and no opening prices, so those cells are blank.
  • Closing numbers: nflverse/nfldata games.csv — the archive ships closing lines, not openers. No official NFL source publishes betting lines, so every price row is community data, flagged UNVERIFIED, and linked to the pinned archive. The NFL schedule link on each row is context only (the game is an NFL game); it is not a source for the line.
  • Blanks are not estimates: an em dash means the value is not in the pinned archive. Nothing here is filled in or inferred.
