Correlations & parlays

Joint probabilities, settlement terms and the limits of independence.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

Correlation is a pricing input, not a decoration

For binary legs A and B, compare the observed joint probability with P(A)P(B). For a parlay, the relevant object is the joint win/push/loss distribution and the actual ticket price. Correlation estimates below are blank in the strict view because the required market lines or player-prop histories have not been officially verified row by row. Earlier community-data estimates are preserved in the research annex.

Lift = P(A ∩ B) / [P(A) P(B)]
Phi = [P(A ∩ B) − P(A)P(B)] / √[P(A)(1−P(A))P(B)(1−P(B))]
Fair parlay EV = Σ joint-outcome probability × net payout

Parlay validation checklist

  1. Use distinct selection identities; never count the same leg twice.
  2. Use a common decision time and simultaneous or otherwise available quotes.
  3. Estimate dependence on training data only, with uncertainty and out-of-sample joint calibration.
  4. Use the actual correlated ticket quote, not a product of standalone prices.
  5. Apply the exact push, void, injury and dead-heat rules.
  6. Stress correlation and tails; pass the same selection-bias gates as single bets.
