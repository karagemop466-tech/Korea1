Measured dependence between every kind of leg — the numbers a parlay or prop pricer actually needs.

Why correlation decides parlay pricing

A parlay's price is only valid if you know how the legs co-move. Multiplying probabilities assumes independence; a same-game parlay almost never satisfies that assumption. Everything below is measured on the pinned settlement data, not imported from a textbook.
