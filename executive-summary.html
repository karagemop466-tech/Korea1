Executive summary

This project asked one question — can we originate our own NFL lines and build strategies that beat the market for reasons other than luck? — and answered it with a reproducible pipeline rather than an opinion. The short version: the lines are good, the strategies are not validated, and the pipeline is able to say so.

1 · What was built

  1. A pinned, hash-verified data layer. One commit of nflverse/nfldata, 7,548 schedule rows, 7,384 played games 1999–2026, plus an independent per-side price file used to cross-check settlement. Every file is SHA-256 hashed into data/sources.json.
  2. A market-free line-origination engine. Three independent model families (weighted ridge on pre-game structure, matchup expected points, Elo) fitted walk-forward and averaged with fixed weights. No model sees a price, and none sees anything after kickoff — both are asserted in code, not just claimed.
  3. A priced, settled strategy library. 18 strategies — single bets, 2- and 3-leg parlays, and classic angle rules — staked one unit per bet at the recorded closing price. No -110 substitution, no line shopping, pushes refunded.
  4. Eight validation gates covering data integrity, leakage, statistical power, significance with FDR control across the library, bootstrap and deflated-Sharpe luck filters, economic significance, season-to-season stability and redundancy.
  5. A static dashboard (this site) where every table row links to the source that produced it, plus a Flagged for review page listing everything that is unverified or contradictory.

2b · The prop module

Player-level play-by-play (2019–2022, 21,033 player-games) is fetched through the GitHub git-blob API and pinned by blob SHA. It produces 9,730 originated prop lines and a 0.66–0.73 AUC on out-of-sample ranking. Leg dependence is measurable: QB passing yards with his own WR1's receiving yards lift the joint hit rate 1.136× over independence, a player's receptions with his own receiving yards lift it 1.446×, and the cross-game control sits at φ = -0.036 with an interval spanning zero. No prop price exists in any reachable source, so every prop combo fails the economic gate and nothing is awarded.

3 · Decisions that shaped the result

  • Bet the close, not the opener. Openers exist for one season only, so every strategy is measured against the hardest available benchmark. This makes results worse and more honest.
  • Declare the library first. All 18 strategies were written down before any of them ran, and every one is published. No result was dropped, no threshold retuned.
  • Correct for the number of tries. Testing 18 strategies at p<0.05 would manufacture a winner about 60% of the time; the Benjamini–Hochberg step and the deflated Sharpe exist to stop that from happening here.
  • Never fill a gap. Prop prices, team-total prices and multi-season openers are unavailable from reachable sources, so they are blank and flagged instead of modelled from proxies. Player-level statistics turned out to be reachable and are now used, which is why the props page has real numbers on it.

4 · What is not proven

Everything on the Flagged for review page, in one sentence each:

  • Player-prop behaviour is measured, but not priceable — the lines, the calibration and the leg correlation are real; the prop price that would turn them into a return is not available anywhere this build can reach, so gate G6 fails for every prop combo.
  • Open-to-close movement is measured for 2021 only.
  • The two nflverse price files disagree by half a point on about a third of games, and three 2015 games have outcome flags that contradict their own score.
  • The model omits injuries, starting-quarterback status and depth charts.
  • Weather is recorded for only two thirds of games, so weather strategies run on a subset.
  • Official-source coverage is partial. An independent audit read 39 games on NFL.com and found 31 scores identical to the pinned data (the other 8 are 2026 week-8 games the pinned snapshot predates); historical betting lines have no official NFL source; and 516 of 523 table rows link to a community mirror rather than an NFL site. See the Independent audit.

5 · The one-paragraph conclusion

The pipeline can originate a defensible NFL line — within about a third of a point of the close, with no market input at all — but that gap is decisive. Once every strategy is forced through power, FDR, bootstrap and deflated-Sharpe gates, the apparent winners dissolve: the best result in the library is a 3-leg parlay ladder that a reality check cannot distinguish from luck (p = 0.186). The honest deliverable is therefore the machinery that can tell the difference, plus a documented list of exactly which data would be needed to go further.
