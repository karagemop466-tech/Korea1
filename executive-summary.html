Executive summary

The decision, the evidence and the limits—without a promised betting edge.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

Decision

Keep the research engine. Do not fund or reward a strategy yet.

The repository contains a working line-origination pipeline, a strategy library, settlement tests, correlation diagnostics and a fail-closed competition. It does not contain the verified, timestamped sportsbook quotes needed to establish an executable edge. No single bet, parlay or player-prop combo has passed every gate. This is an evidence gap, not proof that every strategy has zero expected value.

Corrections to the earlier report

  • The previous headline compared model errors on all games with market errors on a priced subset. The corrected pipeline uses paired samples.
  • The old probability mapping claimed a normal continuity correction priced NFL key numbers correctly. It now uses prior out-of-fold empirical residuals; calibration remains an estimate.
  • The old parlay entries selected the largest edges across an entire week, including later kickoffs. They now select an earliest eligible simultaneous slate.
  • The old “LUCK” label confused failure to reject with proof of luck. Public verdicts now distinguish unverified evidence from validated performance.
  • Earlier results remain available in the research annex for comparison. They are superseded for decision-making, not deleted.

Next evidence needed

  1. Timestamped, bookmaker-published pre-kickoff lines and executable ticket quotes, including limits and settlement terms.
  2. Official game results and timestamped player availability, with archived player statistics and prop settlement rules.
  3. A frozen prospective registry of all strategy trials, followed by untouched paper-trading replication.
  4. Joint prop outcomes and actual SGP prices to test correlation rather than assume independence.
