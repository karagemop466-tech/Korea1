# Executive summary — NFL Line Lab

**Status: research under review, not a verified wagering or reward system.** The existing dashboard explores originated spreads, totals and moneylines, single-bet and parlay strategies, validation gates and correlations. A separate annex explores player-stat relationships. It does not provide verified, executable player-prop prices. **No strategy is certified as skill-based**, and statistical validation cannot eliminate luck.

The original historical analysis reports that no strategy passed its configured gates. This is a provisional code output, not proof of a lack of skill: historical weather imputation uses later data, the leakage gate does not detect it, and the weekly parlay backtest does not establish that all closing prices were simultaneously available. The open board has been disabled because its input snapshot is stale. Correlations describe historical samples, not proof that selected bets are independent.

Official row-by-row verification was not completed. The main feed is the independent [nflverse data project](https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata); the automated checks only test structural sourcing and links. Consult the [NFL schedule](https://www.nfl.com/schedules/) and [official NFL rulebook](https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/2026-nfl-rulebook) for official facts; these do not verify sportsbook prices or derived models. [Flagged for review](flagged.html#audit-2026) and the [audit report](AUDIT_2026-11-02.md) explain the gaps and evidence.

**Decision:** do not award competition rewards or use the dashboard's historical outcomes as live betting recommendations. First obtain primary-source, timestamped prices and outcomes, remove lookahead, rebuild from reproducible inputs, and evaluate preregistered strategies prospectively with uncertainty and multiple-testing controls. Unavailable or unverified market and player-prop values must remain unconfirmed.
