Sources & downloads

Official references, primary methods and hash-pinned research inputs.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

Source hierarchy

  1. Official observations: NFL game results, schedules, rules and player availability.
  2. Primary methods: original paper, publisher or author/university record; a citation does not validate our implementation by itself.
  3. Operator terms: the specific bookmaker's published settlement rules, not universal betting law.
  4. Community research: pinned nflverse and replication archives, with hashes and explicit unverified status.

A source link is not a blanket endorsement. NFL.com does not authenticate the community sportsbook prices.
