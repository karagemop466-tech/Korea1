Independent audit

A second session re-derived every headline number, opened every cited link, and recorded what it found — including three defects it fixed.

What this page is

Every other page on this site reports a result produced by the build. This page is the check on the build. It was written by a session that did not write the analysis (arena/5b3f0799-korea1, 2026-11-02T17:33:07Z), and it re-derives the headline numbers from the committed tables rather than restating them. Where a figure could not be re-derived, it says so instead of passing it.

Two classes of check appear below. Automatic checks run in pipeline/audit.py and fail the build if they disagree. Recorded checks are runs that need the git-ignored raw snapshot (re-running the whole pipeline, the leakage tests, the play-by-play aggregation); they were executed on 2026-11-02 and are described with their command and result, and the provenance hashes they rest on are re-checked automatically.

4 · What could not be checked, and why

  • Individual odds rows. The prices come from a single upstream file. They are checked structurally (sign convention, hold, settlement cross-check) but not row by row against a second price feed, because no second feed exists in this environment.
  • Prop prices. No source in this repository carries a player-prop price, so no prop P&L is claimed anywhere. The measured prop correlations are re-derived and checked (PROP-01).
  • Live 2026 markets. The pinned archives stop before the current season, so the strategy results are historical only.
  • The raw snapshot. data/raw/ is git-ignored because it is 23 MB of re-fetchable data. In CI the provenance checks report "not staged" rather than pretending to pass; they pass in full on a machine that has run make data.
