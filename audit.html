Scope

Independent re-checks of the published tables against official NFL.com and club pages, originally run by session arena/1642ee06-korea1 on 2026-11-02 and refreshed on 2026-11-12 (T14) across all 29 settled Week 8–9 games plus Week 10 injuries and 27/28 official club depth charts. Nothing is inferred: a value is called verified only if it was read on an official NFL or club page, and everything else is listed as unverified below. The NFL.com and club pages were read with the agent's page-fetch tool; the build shell cannot reach them.

Irregularities for review

Irregularities found by the audit
IDSeverityFindingSource
IR-T10-01highThe brief requires every table row to link to an official site. 516 of 523 table rows on the ten root pages link only to github.com/nflverse (a community open-data project, not the NFL); 3 link to nfl.com; 4 carry no external link and are flagged unverified. Previous checkers accepted any external URL, so they passed.NFL official scores · historical inventory
IR-T10-02highHistorical closing lines, totals, moneylines and prices cannot be confirmed from an official NFL source, and the upstream data dictionary does not name the book. Strict public tables now blank these observations; unverified community estimates are retained only in the labelled research annex and data downloads.NFL official scores · community dictionary
IR-T10-03medium · refreshedOn 2026-11-02 the original snapshot was stale with missing Week 8 games. Re-pinned on 2026-11-12 to 9654533e5a09; 0 audited Week 8-9 rows remain blank (all 29 match NFL.com). Remaining gap: 5 Week 10 scheduled QBs in nfldata conflict with official club charts (see injuries.html).NFL.com 2026 week 9
IR-T10-04lowSources page cites https://www.nfl.com/ (the home page) for “GSIS — source of the game IDs” and describes games.csv as a “mirror of NFL GSIS game results”. DATASETS.md supports only that the gsis column is a GSIS id. The 39-game score check supports the scores; it does not support the claim for lines.NFL official scores
IR-T10-05lowThe 59 compared games are a small sample, not a census. Five regular-season weeks were drawn with random.seed(1642) over 2010–2025; three were read (2022 wk 10, 2016 wk 8, 2014 wk 15) and two were not (2022 wk 9, 2011 wk 17) to keep the page-fetch volume down. Added: the 2026 week 8 page and three targeted 2015 games. Only the games visible in the first fetch chunk of each week page were compared. NFL.com uses older team codes for three clubs (STL, OAK, JAC); the script maps them with an explicit alias table.NFL official scores · audit script
IR-T10-06mediumscripts/99_verify.py check V02 failed on every fresh checkout because nothing ever downloaded its reference file (teams_colors_logos.csv). Repaired: the check now fetches it through api.github.com; if that fails the check still fails.NFL official scores · community reference
IR-T10-07infoThe three 2015 games where nflverse closing_lines.csv marks a winner that contradicts the score were checked on NFL.com Game Center: ARI 13–25 PIT, PHI 33–27 DAL (OT), KC 34–14 BAL. games.csv carries the NFL.com score in all three; the outcome flags in closing_lines.csv are the wrong ones.NFL.com Game Center
