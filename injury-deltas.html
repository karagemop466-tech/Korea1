Injury and depth changes between dated captures

Official injury-report and club depth-chart captures, published as changes only. Every change carries both capture times and the raw-page hash.

What this page shows

The injury report and the club depth charts are captured from official pages on a schedule (GitHub Actions, .github/workflows/availability.yml). Each capture keeps its clock time and the sha256 of the raw page. This page publishes only the changes between captures. The levels (how many lines a capture holds) are stored in data/processed/injury_capture_ledger.csv and are not published here, by design.
