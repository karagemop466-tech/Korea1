How a market is originated: the side, then the total, then the moneyline

Download the manifest →

The order is a property of the model, not a presentation choice. The side is originated first because the moneyline is a read-out of the same margin distribution; the total is originated next so that both published lines come from one fit of one training window.

What is still missing, and how it would be found

  • No official price exists. NFL.com publishes scores, injuries and schedules; it does not publish betting lines or prices. Until a bookmaker's own quote is captured with its timestamp and its limits, every market number in this project is class C and every return computed from it is class D.
  • No opening moneyline. The opening-line archive in the pinned upstream covers the spread and the total for the 2021 season and carries no price at all, so the opening number is blank for every other game and for the moneyline everywhere.
  • The official results feed is 30 games. It is the anchor the whole forward record rests on, and it is the first thing to extend: each game page is read, the final is transcribed, and the page URL and the verification time are recorded.
  • Discovery. The three sources above are the only routes to the missing evidence, and each of them is a named, scheduled task rather than an assumption.
