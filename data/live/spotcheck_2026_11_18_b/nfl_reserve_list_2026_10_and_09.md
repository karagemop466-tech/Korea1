# nfl.com reserve list — October and September 2026, read 2026-11-18 ~05:0xZ

These two pages were read to test one hypothesis raised by the November comparison: **is a month page a
complete record, or a fixed-size window?** The November page printed exactly 25 rows and stopped at
11/10 although 11/1–11/9 must have had transactions. If the page is a window, October and September
would each also print 25 rows and stop before the first of the month.

every row and is transcribed blank. Club code `AZ` is printed for the Cardinals (see the November
transcription for the note).

## What the three months together show

| Month page | Complete rows read | Earliest date printed | Latest date printed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-09 | 25 (+1 uncounted split row; the 17th row was cut mid-cell) | 09/26 | 09/30 |
| 2026-10 | 25 | 10/24 | 10/31 |
| 2026-11 | 25 | 11/10 | 11/17 |

Three consecutive months, three identical counts of 25 complete rows, each stopping several days
before the end of its month. That is consistent with a **rolling 25-row window per month page** and
inconsistent with a complete monthly record. It is still an inference from three pages, not a rule
the league prints, so it is recorded as strong evidence rather than as a stated fact.
