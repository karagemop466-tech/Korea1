3 · The one path this review recommends

Prices & executable quotes — proposed by 4 of the registers, 11 proposals, and it holds 8 of the blocked questions.

Why this one. it is the only topic that more than half of the independent registers converge on and that also holds the largest number of blocked questions; nothing downstream can be validated while it is open

What choosing it does not do. it does not spend money, does not approve a reward and does not change any published number.

What it costs. Nothing is required to decide. Executing it is a separate decision with a real cost, and the executive summary already records that the evidence does not support funding anything today.

The reason this is the recommendation is structural rather than statistical: without a timestamped, executable price for a bet, gate G1 fails for every strategy, so a leaderboard built on the current inputs cannot rank anything. The other topics are not less valuable — they are simply downstream of this one.
