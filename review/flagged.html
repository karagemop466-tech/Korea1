The reproducibility baseline is stale - the most important flag

data/processed/audit_digests.csv is the repository's own promise: a SHA-256 for every derived table and every rendered page, with an audit script written to fail when a digest no longer matches. Re-running that comparison today fails on 34 of 50 entries. Two are not cosmetic:

  • strategy_bets.csv: recorded 26,149 rows, now 27,218. The backtest population changed after the baseline was written, and the narrative figure 26,149 in suggestions_review.csv (SUG-03) is no longer reproducible from the file it describes.
  • verification_report.csv: recorded 13 rows (13 pages), now 33. The verifier checks far more pages than the baseline covers, so the baseline cannot certify them.

This is exactly the failure the audit was built to catch: a documented check that no longer runs. The fix is one command (python pipeline/audit.py --rebuild) followed by a review of the diff, but that rewrites published numbers, so it is your call rather than this session's.

What this audit could not check

Three things are outside what a static audit can prove, and are recorded here rather than assumed:

  1. That a number was never available at a better price. Without quotes, no backtest can prove an executable edge. That is the G1 problem, not an audit gap.
  2. That a community-mirrored historical price was ever on offer. A hash proves a file is intact; it does not make the price official.
  3. That the injury report is a complete census. It is the official page as published at capture time; four clubs were absent at an earlier capture and are recorded as absent, not healthy.
