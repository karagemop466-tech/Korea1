Decision matrix - choose a path before changing anything

Every existing proposal scored on one scale, with a recommended six-step sequence.

How to use this page. This is the "review suggestions and choose a path before implementing anything" surface, consolidated onto one scale. Every proposal that already exists in the repository is scored on five criteria, with the sentence the score was read from. Disagree with a score and you can see exactly what to change. Nothing on this page has been implemented.

Why this order. The top of the list is free, reversible and unblocks other work: publish the review surface, keep a dated decision log, run the claim check on every change, and settle the questions that need only your decision. The expensive items (a price feed, licensed participation data) rank lower precisely because they cost money and cannot be undone. Those need your explicit choice, not an agent's.
