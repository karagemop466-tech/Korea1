Injuries, backups and lineup deficiencies

What the official injury report and club depth charts prove, and the five gaps that still block pricing.

What is measured, and what is not. The scheduled tracker reads the official NFL injury report and each club's own depth chart. It records who is listed first, who is listed behind them, and the official game status. It does not know who actually takes the snap, and no point value is attached to any absence. "2nd string" here means "second in the club's published order", which is not the same as "confirmed starter".
