The pairing convention, stated before any number

The matrix lines up groups, because a positional pairing is the only thing two published depth charts can be asked about honestly:

  • RECEIVERS (WR) vs COVERAGE — CORNERS — Outside receiver against the published corner group.
  • RECEIVERS (WR) vs COVERAGE HELP — SAFETIES — Outside receiver against the published safety group (the deep-half the chart lists next).
  • TIGHT ENDS (TE) vs IN-LINE COVERAGE — WEAKSIDE/LB — Tight end against the published weakside/linebacker group.
  • BACKS (RB/FB) vs BOX — INSIDE LINEBACKERS — Backfield against the published inside/box linebacker group.
  • TACKLES (LT/RT) vs EDGE RUSH — Published tackle group against the published edge group (the brief's EDGE1-vs-2nd-string-OT case).
  • INTERIOR LINE (LG/C/RG) vs INTERIOR FRONT — Guards and centre against the published interior front (the brief's 2nd/3rd-string DL case).

This is a convention about which two lists to print side by side. It is not a claim about who aligned against whom on a play, and no snap, route or block data is used: none is captured (see register rows R-24 and R-57).
