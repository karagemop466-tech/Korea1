Open questions

What must be known before a decision can reasonably be made, what we already know, and what is still missing.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

Answer discipline. A question is answered only when a cited source or a reproducible artefact settles it. Otherwise it is open (closable with work we can do), blocked (needs data, a licence or a network we do not have) or needs decision (the owner's call). Blank cells mean the answer is not established — they are never filled with a plausible number.

What the register says in one paragraph

The build is reproducible and its internal checks pass, but the evidence that would justify a bet is missing: there is no authenticated, timestamped price archive (Q09), no league-wide, dated depth ordering (Q30) and no complete trial history (Q23). Everything that depends on those three is therefore blocked, and the strategy leaderboard correctly awards nothing. The fastest honest improvement is not more modelling — it is buying the price evidence (S07) and timestamping availability captures (S16).
