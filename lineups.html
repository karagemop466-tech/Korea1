Automatic collection boundary

The read-only official-source monitor is scheduled daily in GitHub Actions. It archives the NFL injury report and two club depth charts with retrieval times and hashes, and fails on fetch/obvious error-page failures. Its artifacts are not verified extraction, do not update this dated table and do not enter the model. No source credentials or manual data entry are required.

Before a replacement can affect a line: verify identity, as-of roster and position, official inactive status, explicit starting/replacement announcement, prior snap/usage evidence and out-of-sample incremental predictive value. Handle offensive and defensive position groups separately. A missing starter is not itself a quantified deficiency.
