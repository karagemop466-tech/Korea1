Review rule

  1. Enter the suggestion with ID, description, expected decision impact and source.
  2. It moves to “Under review” when a reviewer picks it up.
  3. Record the decision: adopt, adopt with modification, or reject — with rationale and a source link.
  4. Adopted changes are implemented in code, verified (make build && make test), and only then published.
  5. Rejected or deferred suggestions stay visible with their rationale; nothing is silently dropped.
