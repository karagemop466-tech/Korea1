4 · How to choose a path

The proposals do not compete: they form four dependency chains, and the first move in each is cheap and reversible.

  1. Prove the results. RES-01 → RES-02 → CO-01. No new cost; the binding question is the evidence standard (BQ-06). Without it the site can settle 30 games.
  2. Price the market. PS-01 is the only proposal with a subscription cost, and it is the only one that can make G1 pass. Nothing else in the library can be validated until it does.
  3. Test the signal properly. SIG-02 → SIG-01. Free, and it protects the two-test board from becoming a multiple-testing artefact itself (BQ-03).
  4. Make availability a fact. DEP-01 → DEP-02. Free at the capture end; the modelling end should wait for the capture.

The honest recommendation, given the evidence on this site, is to do (1) and (3) first: they cost nothing, they make every existing table more trustworthy, and they do not commit the owner to a subscription. That is a recommendation, not a decision — the decision is recorded in DECISIONS.md when the owner makes it.
