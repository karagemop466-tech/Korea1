Replacement depth

How deep is the man who inherits the slot — from the official injury report joined to official club depth charts.

The measurement, stated precisely

Two official documents are joined on club + player:

  1. the NFL injury report, which states each player's game designation (Out, Doubtful, Questionable, or blank), and
  2. each club's official depth chart, which states an ordered list of names per slot — depth rank 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

The join answers the question the brief asks: when a man is unavailable, how deep is the man who inherits his slot? Not "was there a replacement" but which string is the replacement.

The zero in the 3rd-string row is a finding, not a gap. It says that on the 2026-11-14 capture, no club in this sample had to go three deep at any slot where the top two men were both designated Out. Two earlier versions of this module reported that number wrongly — the first only looked at injured players to decide who was "next", and the second ignored that Green Bay prints a jersey number in front of every charted name. Both defects and their repairs are recorded in COORDINATION.md and fail-closed tests guard each one.

What this page will not say

  • It will not say a man started. A chart rank is a published position. Neither chart carries a publication time, so a rank cannot be tied to a kickoff. Every row records role_evidence = CHART_POSITION_ONLY.
  • It will not convert a designation into a probability. Doubtful and Questionable are published as risk and never mapped to a number.
  • It will not name a replacement where the join failed. 65 injury rows are not on their club's chart — 16 of them from Houston, whose chart published no parseable rows (HOU). Those rows are published as NOT ON CHART, not fuzzy-matched, because a wrong match would mis-state a man's depth.
  • It will not compare a backup to a starter's production. That needs player-level official statistics keyed to the same games, which this project does not hold.

The next step that would make this a predictive signal is stated on the decision questions page: it needs a historical availability archive, and the archive that exists is a single undated snapshot.
