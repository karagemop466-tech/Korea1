How each flagged item was handled in the build

  • Player-level and prop cells are blank, not zero and not estimated. Any table that would need them says so.
  • Opening lines are never inferred. Only the 2021 openers are used, and only where explicitly labelled.
  • The settlement engine uses games.csv for the number and the price, and the cross-check against closing_lines.csv is published with its residual error rate.
  • Weather strategies require a recorded value; a missing value never triggers a bet.
