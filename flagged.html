Prop lines are ours, not a book'smethod limitationdocumentedThe reliability and calibration tests are run against a *benchmark* line — the prior-season league median for the market — not against a sportsbook number. A book's line embeds injury status, snap share and sharp money; ours uses none of those. Discrimination (AUC) and the correlation structure survive that gap; the level of any single line does not.pbp mirrorProp prices would let the tests run against the market.

How each flagged item was handled in the build

  • Prop prices are blank, not assumed. Where a prop price would be needed (any economic claim) the table says so and the gate fails; the measured correlation and calibration do not need one.
  • The player-level data that an earlier build marked unreachable is fetched, hashed and used. The old entry is kept above, marked CORRECTED, rather than deleted.
  • Opening lines are never inferred. Only the 2021 openers are used, and only where explicitly labelled.
  • The settlement engine uses games.csv for the number and the price, and the cross-check against closing_lines.csv is published with its residual error rate.
  • Weather strategies require a recorded value; a missing value never triggers a bet.

Scope and jurisdiction

This site is research output. It places no bets, offers no betting service, holds no client money and makes no claim about the legality of any market in any jurisdiction. It says measured dependence and validated or not, not bet this. Rules on remote gambling differ by country and by operator, and a positive measured correlation is not permission to act on it — check your own jurisdiction's rules and the operator's published terms before using anything here.
