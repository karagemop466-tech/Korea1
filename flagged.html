What to check first when reviewing

  1. IR-01/IR-02: the closing-line provenance. If the upstream book is not the book you actually bet into, expect the ROI column to shift.
  2. IR-03: props. If you can supply a verified prop-line history, the prop layer becomes backtestable without any other change — the correlation and calibration work is already done.
  3. IR-06: line movement. One season of opening lines is not a sample.
