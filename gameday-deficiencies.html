What this page is

Every row is one player who carries a game designation on the official NFL.com injury report, joined to the position group on his own club’s published depth chart. “String” is the club’s order, not a projection. “Next listed” is the name the club prints immediately below him at that position group — the player who has to absorb the snaps if the designation holds.

  • Official: player, injury, practice status and game status — nfl.com/injuries.
  • Club-published (most clubs label it “unofficial”): the 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th string order and the next listed player, linked per row to the club’s own depth-chart page.
  • Computed here: the join, the string label and the deficiency tier. Nothing is inferred: an absent chart row prints a blank string and a blank next man up, counted as Unmatched on the club chart.
  • Still missing from every capture: the official game-day inactives filed 90 minutes before kickoff under the NFL Game Operations rules. Until those are captured, an Out designation is the strongest available evidence — it is not confirmation of who actually played.
