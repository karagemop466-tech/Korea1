## Findings requiring integration review

1. **Official-source requirement is not established.** The live leaderboard links derived ROI, p-values and other outputs to [nflverse's games.csv](https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata/blob/a270278f3ac4e937295fe6a27814b3c4a2b62645/data/games.csv), a third-party dataset, not an official NFL or sportsbook source that directly verifies those derived outputs. `tests/check_sources.py` checks only for an HTTP link or marker; it does not establish source authority, check each cell, or even enforce that flagged values are blank. Thus its success message is stronger than what it tests. Line-by-line official verification is not demonstrated. A full row/cell audit and blanking or flagging of unverified values are required.
2. **Statistical claims are overstated.** The [live home page](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/Korea1/) says gates “remove luck,” labels nonsignificant positive returns “LUCK,” and says independent pricing is fine for one pair based on a small correlation. Non-rejection does not prove luck, zero correlation does not establish independence, and model assumptions and uncertainty matter for parlay pricing. `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md` similarly asserts a causal explanation for the model-market gap without establishing it. These need qualified language.
3. **Reproduction incomplete.** In a fresh checkout `python3 scripts/99_verify.py` returned 8/9: V02 failed because its reference file is absent in ignored raw staging. The verifier also modifies `data/lab/verification.json`; that incidental change was restored. No full ingest or build was run. Passing other checks does not establish a clean rebuild or line-by-line verification.
4. **Props not validated.** The [flagged page](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/Korea1/flagged.html) reports no retrieved prop price feed. A profitable or skill-based player-prop combination cannot be established on that basis.
