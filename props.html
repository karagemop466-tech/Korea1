Build protocol

  1. Archive official player statistics and reconstruct the information available before kickoff.
  2. Use stable player IDs and franchise mappings; account for inactive players and role changes.
  3. Record the exact prop threshold, price, timestamp, bookmaker and settlement terms.
  4. Model the joint distribution of legs, including pushes and voids; calibrate on untouched games.
  5. Compare expected value with the actual SGP quote, then run all eight gates.
