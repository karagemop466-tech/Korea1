Player props

A source-first framework for player-prop combinations; no fabricated prices or returns.

Evidence boundary. Official NFL/club observations, model estimates and community-market research are separated. No invented prop edge

Player-prop combinations are a research category, not a validated product. Four hash-pinned community play-by-play blobs (2019–2022, 188,018 plays / 21,033 player-games) power exact season-total definition checks and 9,730 originated research prop lines, while 2023–2026 logs and authenticated prop/SGP ticket prices remain absent. No prop ROI is claimed.

Build protocol

  1. Archive official player statistics and reconstruct pre-kickoff injury/depth information (see Injuries & depth).
  2. Use stable player IDs and franchise mappings; account for inactive players and backup cascades.
  3. Record the exact prop threshold, price, timestamp, bookmaker and settlement terms.
  4. Model the joint distribution of legs, including pushes and voids; calibrate on untouched games.
  5. Compare expected value with the actual SGP quote, then run all eight gates.
