What this page does not show

These are stated as prose, not as a sourced table, because two of them are internal policy and have no official source to cite. Writing them as table rows would imply an official reference that does not exist.

  • Reward eligibility — none. No strategy has a verdict, so no reward can be awarded. The competition rules require every gate to pass and three are unevaluated (→ competition rules in this repository). The statistical reason a short record cannot earn a verdict is the minimum-track-record-length result in Bailey & López de Prado (2014).
  • A profit claim — none. Two of four tickets won and two lost, which is indistinguishable from chance. Data-snooping over many candidate strategies inflates the best observed result, so a leaderboard is only meaningful after multiplicity control White (2000).
  • Executable prices — not verified. The frozen prices came from the community mirror, not from authenticated bookmaker quotes, which is why gate F6 fails for every strategy. No official source publishes a historical record of executable quotes, so this cannot be fixed by citing one.
  • Injury context for these picks — not joined. The official injury report was captured on 2026-11-12, after the picks were frozen, and is published separately (NFL.com injury report). Joining it to these picks now would be hindsight, so it is not done.
