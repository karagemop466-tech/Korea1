Requirement annex · row-by-row evidence and official re-read

Every line of the brief, one row each: what is built, what proves it, what blocks it, and the number that would clear it — plus the eight decisions only the owner can take.

What this page is

This is the annex to the 2026-11-18 request register. The register is the primary surface: one row per request item, hand-checked against the artefacts on main. This annex adds the three things a register cannot carry on its own: every number in it is recomputed from a committed file on each build rather than written once; the official pages it relies on were re-read on 2026-11-18 and compared cell by cell with the committed captures; and the questions and owner decisions are separated into their own registers so a decision can be taken without re-reading seventy pages.

Where the two disagree, both are printed. The one known disagreement is the removal-of-luck row: the register calls it DONE because the panel is published, this annex calls it PARTIAL because no strategy is ranked by a proper score (Brier skill) even though 44 model rows carry one. R14 below says so in the evidence column.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

How to read a row. Status is one of DONE (a committed artefact does the thing), PARTIAL (a real artefact exists and a named part is missing), BLOCKED (nothing in the repository can close it: it needs an official source that does not exist yet, or an owner decision), or REJECTED AS REQUESTED (the brief asked for something the evidence says must not be done; the rejection is recorded). Number that clears it is the readable test, not a threshold invented here: it is the value the frozen configuration (validation_config.json) or the next official capture would have to produce. No cell is filled by hand; every number in the evidence column is recomputed from the committed file named in the same row, and tests/test_requirement_annex_2026_11_18.py fails the build if a row loses its source or its clearing number.
