Every published row re-checked against the official page, one line at a time.

What this page is. An independent second check of the published tables against the official NFL sources, run outside the build. The build's own verifier only proves that every row carries an official link; this page checks whether the row matches it. Where the two disagree, the row is listed here instead of being corrected silently.

1 · What was checked, and how

The build sandbox shell cannot complete a TLS handshake with nfl.com (this was re-measured on 2026-11-13 and still fails), so the official pages were read with the agent page-fetch tool and the exact cells were transcribed into committed files under data/live/. That makes the comparison reproducible from a clean checkout without network access, and it makes the transcription itself auditable.

2 · The control: is the published parser faithful?

Before blaming the build for any difference, the build's parser was checked against a document that cannot drift. The repository already holds a verbatim capture of the same official page taken 2026-11-12T13:06:29Z, and the availability workflow's own snapshot from the same day. Both describe the same instant, so any difference between them would be a parser defect.

3 · Injury table, field by field

Every published cell for the eight transcribed clubs was compared with the official page. Differences are classified so that a normal midweek status update is never reported as an error:

  • confirmed defect — the published value cannot be explained by a later update, e.g. a player the table itself recorded as Out shown as a full practice participant.
  • unresolved — the player is on the official page but missing from the snapshot; a status added between capture times and a parser drop both fit, so it is not asserted as either.
  • drift possible — the values differ and practice statuses are published in stages, so an update between the two capture times explains it.
  • no basis — the official cell is blank; nothing can be confirmed.

10 · What this page does not verify

The limits are stated here rather than left for the reader to assume:

  • Sample, not census. Four of the sixteen week-10 matchups and four of the 32 clubs were transcribed. The other clubs are outside the verified sample and are not graded, not even implicitly.
  • The transcription is manual. It is a human reading of the official page. The control in section 2 shows the build's parser is faithful, but that does not make the transcription infallible; every disagreeing cell is published above for manual re-check.
  • No prices. Nothing on this page touches a sportsbook price, a limit or a timestamped quote. Gate G1 remains failed for every strategy regardless of what is verified here.
  • Order is not role. A depth chart places names in an order. It does not confirm who starts, who is active, or how much a backup will play; only the official inactives list and the game itself do that.
  • No causal claim. Verifying that a player is out says nothing about how many points that is worth. No availability term has been measured or priced.
