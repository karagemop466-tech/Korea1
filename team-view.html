How to read it

  • Starter = the first-listed player of a depth-chart slot, as the club's own chart prints it. A slot can list more than one player at rank 1; this page never names one of them as 'the' starter of a position.
  • Replacement named = the next player the same slot lists, with his printed rank and game status. Blank means the chart names no one.
  • Net injury count = Out + Doubtful + Questionable entries on the report. It counts entries, not lost value.
  • Out, Doubtful, Questionable are the printed Game Status values; the Questionable label is not calibrated (see the open-questions page).
