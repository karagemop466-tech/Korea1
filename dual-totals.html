Two total estimators

The published game total, a second independent estimate, the gap, and the pre-registered walk-forward verdict.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

What this page publishes

The brief asks to publish every model total twice until one wins: keep both estimators visible with the gap column, and only collapse to one after a walk-forward comparison (REQ-62 / RR-62). This page is that comparison. It is not a strategy, not a leaderboard row and not a validation-gate change: backtest numbers never enter the leaderboard by project rule, and nothing here awards or demotes anything.

The two estimators

A — the published total. pred_total_ensemble, the equal-weight ensemble of the ridge, matchup and Elo totals already published as fair_total on Originated lines. A is not retrained, reweighted or reselected here.

B — the second estimate. The mean of each team's up-to-five most recent regular-season combined scores strictly before the game (league-mean fallback when a team has no prior game). Zero fitted parameters; the window, the averaging and the fallback are fixed constants.

Pre-registration. The rule text was committed to git before the evaluation code existed: research/dual-totals-prereg.md, registration commit b364c23706a4, rule-text sha256 4bc158f9f3af8cb2a560c10fdad163c52b3c7c5a7e07a96ff206200198b0fb43 (the page prints the commit and fingerprint it honoured; a later edit of the rule would create a new registration and leave this one in history). The verdict rule: the paired 95% t-interval of |errorB| − |errorA| per game; the pooled interval is the primary gate, per-season rows are descriptive; collapsing to one total additionally requires the pooled winner to take at least two-thirds of the seasons.

Walk-forward comparison · one row per season

Both estimators are scored on the identical game rows against the final combined score. ΔMAE is B's mean absolute error minus A's, so a positive ΔMAE means A was closer. Every row carries the official NFL source for that season; outcome reads name how many finals are on a committed official read versus the pinned community archive.

Verdict, collapse state and evidence tiers

  • Pooled verdict: A wins — MAE A 10.76 against MAE B 11.07 over 5,819 games; seasons taken by A: 9, by B: 0, no separation: 14.
  • Collapse state: no collapse — A wins pooled gate but no season supermajority. The collapse itself is an owner decision recorded in decision readiness; this build executes none (executed = false).
  • Evidence tiers: 31 of 5,819 evaluated finals sit on a committed official NFL read (the game-centre / score-audit reads); the other 5,788 are pinned-community finals whose settlement is unverified. While any evaluated row is community-only the verdict prints PROVISIONAL, and the official links on every row are there for manual review.
  • Limitations, stated: the interval is the pre-registered game-level paired t-interval (games within a week are not independent, so the interval is optimistic and is kept as registered rather than re-derived); B shares no fitted parameters with A but both are graded on the same outcomes; 2026 is a partial season (108 games at capture).
