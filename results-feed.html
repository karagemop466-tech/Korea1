Verified results feed

Official finals, per game, with the URL and read time behind every score — and nothing filled in.

What this page is. Requirement 2 of the 2026-11-14 brief: a verified results feed so a backtest and an ROI can be computed at all. A result enters the feed only from an official NFL page, with the URL, the page it was read from and the UTC time of the read. A game without an official final is blank and labelled; it is never filled from the community mirror that the historical backtests use.

1 · Why this was blocking everything

An ROI is (units won − units staked) ÷ units staked. Every term needs a settled outcome, and a settled outcome needs a final score. Before this feed the forward ledger had 65 picks and 30 official finals, so 35 picks were OPEN and no strategy scoreboard could be computed. The feed does not create finals that do not exist; it makes the ones that do exist machine-readable, per-game, with their source, and it states exactly how many markets that is: 3 settleable market rows out of 450.

2 · The feed, 2026 season

Settlement is derived from the pinned closing line against the official final, per market. It is a derivation, not an observation: it says whether the closing number would have won, lost or pushed — not that any bet was placed at that price. Pushes are named as pushes; a blank closing line cannot win and is shown blank.

4 · The two evidence classes in this feed

  • DATA_EXTRACT — the committed official 2026 extract official_scores_2026_week08_10.csv, read from the NFL.com week pages listed under each row. This is the class a settlement may use.
  • OFFICIAL_RESULTS_REGISTER — the 30 rows already settled by earlier sessions in verified_results.csv (the register rows are merged into it), each with its own NFL.com game URL and read time. Kept as a second source rather than re-transcribed.
  • PINNED_SCHEDULE_PENDING — a game the pinned archive lists for 2026 with no official final yet. It is a placeholder for scheduling, not a result.

What the feed cannot do. It cannot settle a futures market, a player prop, or a game whose official page has not been read. Those stay blank and are listed on Flagged for review. Reading every 2026 game page is a scheduled job, not a manual one — see suggestion RES-01 on the suggestions page.

5 · Full-history join (1999–2026) and archive spot re-reads

The section above settles 2026 from official reads. Backtests and ROI that span every season join on game_id against the full-history feed (results_feed.csv): 7,548 games in three tiers that are never mixed — 30 official game-centre reads, 7383 hash-verified pinned-archive finals (each flagged not re-read per row), and 135 scheduled games whose scores stay blank by design. Cross-checks between the pin and an official read: 1 AGREE, 0 CONFLICT. The archive tier is a research input, not a settlement source: settlement still requires an official read, exactly as section 4 says.

18 pinned rows were re-read on the live official scores pages during the 2026-11-14 integration session — nine games from 2004 week 1 and nine from 2019 week 15, two eras, every score identical to the pin. The register is committed as results_feed_spot_checks.csv.
