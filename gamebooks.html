1 · What the capture actually read

Every official document this repository holds, with the bytes that were read and their SHA-256. The requested week and the published week are separate columns on purpose: the league's schedule application does not always serve the week the URL names, and a capture that silently labelled one week's fixtures with another week's number would corrupt every downstream join.

3 · The availability graph: who was expected to start, who actually did

The expectation is the player the club itself lists first on its own official depth chart. The actual starter is the player the league names in the Game Book's starting lineup. A position where the club lists more than one first player is published as a co-starter row: a club that names three receivers at rank one has not named a starter, and the graph does not choose one.

4 · The league's own inactive and did-not-play lists
