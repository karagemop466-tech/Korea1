# Suggestions register — NFL Line Lab

Source of truth for the **Suggestions** section. `pipeline/build_review.py` parses this file into
`data/processed/suggestions.csv`; `pipeline/render_review.py` renders `suggestions.html`. Edit the
markdown, rebuild, do not edit the CSV.

Columns: `id · area · suggestion · why_it_matters · evidence_we_have · evidence_missing ·
how_to_verify · cost_risk · priority · status · linked_question · source_url`

Statuses: `Proposed · Under review · Accepted · Rejected · Implemented`.
Priorities: `P0 blocking · P1 high · P2 medium · P3 low`.

Nothing here is a recommendation to bet. Suggestions describe what would make the *decision* better.
