Read this as reporting, not recovery. The official report is a weekly instrument: a designation printed on Week 10 and absent on Week 11 shows the designation was not carried forward — the report does not state, and this page does not claim, that the player is healthy. Of the moves above, 78 are a Week 10 designation that is simply absent on Week 11. That is why the count of "changes" is large and why a level taken from one week cannot be used as a signal.

Only players on both captures are compared, so nothing is inferred for a player who appeared or disappeared. The two capture clock times are 2026-11-18T04:24:05Z (Week 10) and 2026-11-19T12:32:32Z (Week 11). A designation that moved is a delta, not a level: the direction is only meaningful against a dated earlier read, which is why both are printed.
