What is deliberately absent

There is no official source for historical NFL betting lines, and none exists: the league does not publish prices and no sportsbook publishes a licence-clean price history. That gap is the reason every odds-derived row is labelled third-party and carries an explicit verification note. It is also why no value on this site is described as "official odds".

Research artifact. No betting advice, no live wagering, no guarantee of profit. Values that could not be confirmed against a source are marked unverified and listed on the Flagged for review page.
