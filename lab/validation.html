Luck removal, measurement 1 — signal persistence

Correlating every strategy's in-sample ROI (2012–2017) with its held-out ROI (2018–2021) across the battery gives r = 0.485. There is some persistent structure in these families — the rules are not pure noise — but not enough for a single one to clear the gate set. Median season-overdispersion luck ratio across the battery: 1.723 (≈1 means season-to-season swings are fully explained by sampling noise).

This is the most important table in the project. A genuinely skilled bettor is invisible on a profit leaderboard and obvious on a closing-line-value board. Any scoring system that ranks on P&L is measuring luck.
