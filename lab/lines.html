The answer is no, and that is the useful result. A results-only model lands within about 0.3 points of the closing spread and then loses the head-to-head on every accuracy metric. Any strategy that simply bets the model's disagreement with the close inherits that error — which is exactly what the strategy table measures.

Model specification

ComponentSpecificationSource
EloK = 20, margin-of-victory multiplier ln(|margin|+1)·2.2/(0.001·Δelo+2.2), home field 48 Elo, season regression ⅓ toward 1500FTE-ELO
Elo → spreadOLS on prior seasons only; slope is estimated from this dataset, not assumed (≈0.04 points per Elo point, i.e. 25 Elo ≈ 1 point)SBR-10Y
