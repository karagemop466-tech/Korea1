Reading the results without fooling yourself

Three patterns matter more than any single row.

  • The vig is 4.55% and almost everything loses about that much. Strategies with ROI near −4.5% are not "close to profitable"; they are behaving exactly like a coin flip paying a tax. S04 (−2.7%), S18 (−3.3%) and S05 (−3.5%) are inside the noise band.
  • Model-edge rules lose more than the vig. S01, S02, S10, S15 and S17 all sit between −3.6% and −11.1%. The market is not merely better than the model; the model's disagreements are a usable signal against it.
  • The two best rows fail on sample size, not on direction. S12 (+5.8% on 226 bets) and S11 (+0.9% on 301 bets) are on the Flagged-for-review list as "watch, do not fund" — 226 bets cannot support a 5.8% claim (see the power arithmetic on the validation page).
