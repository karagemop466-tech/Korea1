How a line is originated

The model predicts home margin and game total from prior results, rolling team scoring, Elo and rest. Ridge regularization is selected inside each historical training window. The equal-weight ensemble never receives a market price or weather/roof observation as an input. Probabilities use prior out-of-fold empirical discrete score residuals, retaining NFL key-number mass and pushes. A theoretical margin is not an executable sportsbook offer or an injury-adjusted quote (see Injuries & depth for Week 10 QB/starter exclusions).

Home handicap = −predicted home margin
Fair decimal price = 1 / predicted win probability
Theoretical two-way decimal price = fair decimal / (1 + overround)
Expected profit = P(win) × (decimal − 1) − P(loss)
