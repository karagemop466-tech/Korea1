Originated lines

A market-free model with explicit estimation and verification boundaries.

Evidence boundary. Official observations, model estimates and community-market research are separate. Unverified observations are blank in the public tables, not filled in. Reproducible estimates remain in the clearly labelled research annex and downloadable CSVs; they are not betting recommendations.

How a line is originated

The model predicts home margin and game total from prior results, rolling team scoring, Elo and rest. Ridge regularization is selected inside each historical training window. The equal-weight ensemble never receives a market price as an input. Probabilities use prior out-of-fold empirical errors, including ties and pushes. A theoretical margin is not an executable sportsbook offer.

Home handicap = −predicted home margin
Fair decimal price = 1 / predicted win probability
Theoretical two-way decimal price = fair decimal / (1 + overround)
Expected profit = P(win) × (decimal − 1) − P(loss)

Moneyline probabilities condition on a non-tie when quoting a two-way push-settled market. These are mathematical definitions, not claims of a profitable price.

The model's line, the market's line and the difference - three separate columns

The three numbers are published in three columns and are never merged into a single "edge" figure, because they answer different questions and carry different evidence. The model's fair line is our own forecast. The market's line is what the market quoted. The difference is what the model thinks the market is wrong by.
