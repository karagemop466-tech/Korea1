Originated lines

A market-free ensemble line, walked forward season by season, measured against the closing market.

The originated line

The published line is an equal-weight ensemble of three model families. All three see only pre-kickoff information: Elo with margin-of-victory updates, opponent-adjusted offence/defence ratings solved weekly by ridge regression, rest, venue, roof and recorded weather. None of them sees a price.

Open board — lines we would post now

Fitted on every completed game in the pinned file, the same ensemble prices the scheduled games. Where the market already has a number, the edge column shows the disagreement; where it does not, this is pure origination. Unplayed games have no recorded kickoff weather, so the model uses the median of the completed games for temperature and wind (fitted on the training data, never on the game being priced) and keeps the temp_missing / wind_missing flags at 1 — the assumption is listed on the Flagged for review page.
