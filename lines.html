Originated lines

A market-free ensemble line, walked forward season by season, measured against the closing market.

The originated line

The published line is an equal-weight ensemble of three model families. All three see only pre-kickoff information: Elo with margin-of-victory updates, opponent-adjusted offence/defence ratings solved weekly by ridge regression, rest, venue, roof and recorded weather. None of them sees a price.
