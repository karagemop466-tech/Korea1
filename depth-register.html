What this register can and cannot answer

It answers
  • Which reported players carry an official game designation, and at which position.
  • Whether a ruled-out player is a listed starter or a backup on his club's published chart.
  • Who the club lists next in that same column — 2nd, 3rd or 4th string.
  • Which rows cannot be classified at all, because the player is absent from the chart.
It does not answer yet
  • Who actually started. Club charts are projections; three clubs call theirs unofficial.
  • League-wide deficiencies: 26 clubs are outside the pilot.
  • How much a deficiency is worth in points. The line model has no availability input.
  • Prop availability. No verified prop prices exist in this repository.

Next step that would settle the first gap: archive official pre-kickoff inactives and post-game snap counts. See Questions Q04 and Suggestions S03.
