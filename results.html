One settlement-grade outcome per game, each with the official document it was read from.

Why a results feed is the prerequisite, not a nicety

A backtest is a claim of the form "this rule, applied to these decisions, would have produced this return". Two inputs are required and they fail independently: the price at decision time (that is prices.html) and the settled outcome (this page). Without an official outcome the ROI denominator is unknown, and an archive score is not an official observation.

The feed lists every played game in the pinned archive — including the 7,382 whose official score has not been read. Dropping them would silently shrink every backtest denominator and inflate every ROI.

The tiers, and the discovery that forced them

During this session the official Game Center pages for three games were fetched directly: commanders-at-giants-2026-reg-10, cardinals-at-rams-2014-reg-15 and bengals-at-falcons-2026-reg-9. All three rendered "Hidden Score" instead of a number, because the score is injected client-side. A feed that transcribed page text would have published "Hidden Score", and a feed that guessed would have been inventing a result.

The same three Game Centers link a National Football League Game Summary PDF — the Game Book. Those PDFs are machine-readable and print the score in a quarter-by-quarter table. Every score in the top tier below was read from that document, with the PDF URL kept as the evidence link.

What this unlocks, and what it still does not

  • Unlocked: for these 31 games, margin, total, winner and push can now be settled against an official document instead of a community transcription.
  • Still blocked: a backtest over 31 games is not a backtest. The 20-year history the model is fitted on has no per-game official score linked, only the archive's transcription. Linking one official Game Book per historical game is mechanical but is 7,300+ document reads, and the rate limit is the agent's page budget, not the data.
  • Still blocked: ROI needs both a price and a result. The results side is solved for 31 games; the price side is solved for zero. Both must be solved before a return is computable at all.

The concrete next step is stated as a question with a cost on the decision questions page rather than acted on unilaterally here.
