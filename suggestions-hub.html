This page is an index, not a fifth register. The repository holds 76 proposals and 90 questions written across eight parallel registers by sessions that could not see each other. Every proposal and question keeps its own page; this page puts the whole choice on one screen: how much is still waiting on you, what gate stands in front of each topic, and what single number or artefact would clear it. 5 · How a path is chosen, and what it costs

  • A path is not a batch approval. Choosing a cluster opens its rows on the register page; each row still has to pass its own gate before anything is implemented, and the gate is named on the row.
  • Two paths cost money — an executable price archive and a paid data feed. They are marked on suggestions.html and no session has spent anything: no stake, prize or allocation exists.
  • A rejected proposal stays published. Rejection is recorded with its reason so it is not silently re-proposed by a later session.
  • What this page cannot tell you: which path is best for your own risk appetite. It gives the sizes, the gates and the cost; the choice is yours, which is exactly what DECISIONS.md records a decision as.
