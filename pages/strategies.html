Line construction

Forecast first. Convert to a price second.

These identities explain the proposed pricing layer; no value can be evaluated until a calibrated probability and an actual, timestamped offered price are available.

Single binary outcome · fair decimal price

dfair = 1 / P(A)

Use the modeled event probability at the exact market threshold. This is the no-margin fair decimal price under the stated binary/settlement definition. Odds-derived probability methods ↗

Single outcome · expected net per 1 unit

EV = p(d − 1) − (1 − p) = pd − 1

Assumes a settled win/loss with no fee, push, void or settlement adjustment. Use the actual quoted price and amend the calculation for the market's rules. NFL lines study (historical context) ↗

Combo · joint event price

dfair = 1 / P(A ∩ B)

Estimate the joint probability directly or from a validated dependence model. Do not use a product of marginals without a supported independence assumption. NIST correlation definition ↗

For a market reference, preserve every side's quote and the pre-registered de-vig method; published odds-conversion methods are not interchangeable by assumption. See the publisher paper ↗. No implied probabilities or fair odds are published here because no licensed price snapshot or calibrated forecast is present.

Recommended sequence

Build the distribution before building the ticket.

The league’s own Next Gen Stats material documents tracking-derived metrics and probability distributions for specific play outcomes. That establishes feature/target possibilities—not betting profitability.

01 · Ingest

Freeze the as-of view

Record source, event, player/team ID, season/week, quote timestamp, extraction time and final-stat revision. Keep historical snapshots immutable.

02 · Forecast

Estimate a distribution

Start with a simple baseline, add features one family at a time and calibrate only on data not used to fit the base model.

03 · Decide

Price, or pass

Require a recorded price, settlement rule and uncertainty-aware edge. A model can return “no bet” whenever inputs, calibration or price quality fail.
