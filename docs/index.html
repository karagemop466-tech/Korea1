Executive summary

Objective. Estimate fair probabilities from information available at a recorded decision time, compare them to a contemporaneous market price, and evaluate performance out of sample.

Selective skill. Separate model selection from evaluation; use rolling time-based holdouts, baselines, calibration, uncertainty intervals, and prospective paper tracking. Report net returns after the actual price and settlement rules, not accuracy alone.

Correlation. Parlays require a joint probability, not multiplication of marginal probabilities when legs are dependent. Estimate dependence from time-separated data; publish the estimate, sample size, uncertainty, and market/season context. No correlation value is currently confirmed.

Reward leaderboard. Rank only strategies that pass the same gates. Publish sample size, net ROI, calibration, drawdown, uncertainty, and date window alongside rank. Do not reward raw hit rate or a short lucky streak.

Recommendation. Begin with single-market probabilities; add same-game and player-prop combos only after joint calibration and settlement validation. Do not deploy or claim an advantage until all inputs and gates pass.
