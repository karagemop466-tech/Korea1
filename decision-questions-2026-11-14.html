What we know, what we do not, and what it would take to close each gap — before any decision.

What this page is for

A decision cannot be made on information that has not been separated from information that is assumed. Every row below separates three things: what we have, what we do not have, and what it would take to close the gap. The answer cell is blank when there is no answer on record — a blank is the honest state, and filling it in with a plausible guess is the failure this register exists to prevent.

The four questions that actually decide the rest

  1. Q-02 — is any betting use intended? This single sentence reorders every other priority. An evidence dashboard should stop buying data; a betting product cannot start without it.
  2. Q-01 — what is the undated price? Every return figure in the project is downstream of this. It is free to ask and it may invalidate published numbers, which is exactly why it is first.
  3. Q-03 — what is the budget? Three paid proposals cannot be ranked without a figure.
  4. Q-13 — which path? The letter chosen on suggestions-hub.html determines which of the other thirteen rows matter next month.

Why so many answers are blank. The remaining uncertainty is not a research gap that more analysis could close. Seven of the fourteen rows are owner decisions or third-party data. The most useful thing this page can do is make that visible, so effort is not spent modelling around a question that only the owner can answer.
