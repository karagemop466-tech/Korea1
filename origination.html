Origination order & fair / market / difference

Side, then total, then moneyline — with the model's fair number, the market number and the difference as separate columns.

What this page is. Requirements 3 and 4 of the 2026-11-14 brief. The model originates in a fixed order — side, then total, then moneyline — and for each market the model's fair number, the market's number and the difference are published as three separate columns. The difference is arithmetic; it is not an edge. An edge column is shown next to it and reads no wherever the archive carries no executable price.

The moneyline is not a spread. American prices are non-linear, so the moneyline difference is computed on hold-free implied probabilities and printed in probability points. Its fair price is derived from the same margin distribution as the side, which the next section states again because it limits what the number can claim.

2 · Out-of-sample accuracy: fair number vs market number

The fair number is the walk-forward ensemble prediction: it is fitted only on seasons before the one it predicts, so this is not an in-sample fit. The market number is the closing line. Both are scored against the same rows, so the gap is paired and not an artefact of different samples.

A negative gap means the model's number was closer to the result than the market's closing number on these rows. That is not a profitable strategy: the model number was not available at a price, and the market close is the hardest number to beat. It is evidence about the model, not about a bet.

4 · What the order buys you

  • Side first because the margin distribution is the best-identified quantity in the data: it is re-used by the total and the moneyline, so an error here propagates to both.
  • Total second because it is a separate distribution (points scored, not points differential) and the pinned archive's total prices are the sparsest of the three.
  • Moneyline last because it is a monotone transform of the margin distribution after a tie adjustment: originating it first would double-count the same information.
