Reward ladder

Points are competition points. They are not money, not a payout obligation and not a performance claim. A tier is only reached by satisfying the requirement and the gate set underneath it; the ladder does not skip. The highest tier that can currently be reached is T0, because gate G1 fails for every candidate.

Removing luck: the eight-gate battery

A profitable backtest is not a finding. Each gate below removes one specific way a backtest can look skilled while being variance, hindsight or selection. The battery is fail-closed: a candidate that fails one gate is UNVERIFIED, never "promising".

The fail-closed validation battery and the statistic that enforces each gate
GateNameWhat it is meant to stopStatistic usedPrimary reference
G1Data provenanceAuthenticated, timestamped, executable quotes with limitsOfficial quote coverage and quote-timestamp coverage are both 0.00White (2000), Econometric Society
G2LeakageEvery feature must exist before the decision time it is used atLeakage test suite (weather fill, season-ahead fit, whole-week parlay selection, future lines)NFL scores
G3Power / sample sizeMinimum bets, seasons and weeks, plus the weeks the observed effect needsMinimum detectable effect at 80% power with weekly blocksNFL scores
G4Significance under multiplicityFamily-wise error controlled across the whole library, not per strategyStationary weekly-block bootstrap with Holm step-down correctionPolitis & Romano (1994), publisher
G5Luck / selectionA best-of-many result must beat the distribution of the maximumWhite's Reality Check bootstrap and a provisional deflated Sharpe ratioWhite (2000), Econometric Society
G6Economic realityThe edge must survive the vig it would actually payAdverse-price stress of five American cents per original legFanDuel push settlement rules
G7StabilityThe edge cannot live in one season or one eraShare of seasons positive, recent-era return, minimum bets per seasonNFL scores
G8RedundancyThe leaderboard should hold distinct signals, not one signal eighteen timesMaximum pairwise bet overlap across the libraryBenjamini & Hochberg (1995), university record

Sample size is the binding constraint. At the configured 80% power and 2% target return, the candidates need between 2,637 and 181,824 weeks of observations; the library has between 122 and 414. No candidate is close. That is a statement about the arithmetic of power, not a judgement about any strategy.

The library-wide reality check already ran. Across 18 strategies and 475 calendar weeks with 3,000 stationary block-bootstrap resamples (mean block 4 weeks, seed 20261101), the observed best mean return does not beat the best-of-many distribution: p = 0.4319. On this evidence the library as a whole has not been shown to outperform zero. White (2000), Econometric Society The deflated Sharpe ratio is provisional: the complete exploratory search history is unknown, so only a lower bound of 60 trials is assumed. Bailey

Related reading: Politis & Romano (1994), publisher for the block bootstrap · Benjamini & Hochberg (1995), university record for the multiplicity correction · FanDuel push settlement rules for settlement terms · the full gate configuration · correlation and parlay settlement.
