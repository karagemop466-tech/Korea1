Proposed rules

Rank only gate-passing submissions by a preregistered out-of-sample proper scoring rule relative to the same baseline and horizon. Publish uncertainty, sample count and economic results separately; do not rank by raw win rate or short-run return alone. Use a locked submission cutoff, identical data availability, full trial registry and an untouched final evaluation. No reward amount, sponsor or award exists yet; prize eligibility and tie rules require a published competition charter.
