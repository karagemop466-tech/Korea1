Research category · 02

Earn eligibility through gates, not anecdotes.

A strategy advances only when its data, forecast, pricing and uncertainty are reproducible. These are proposed project rules; none has passed yet.

“Luck-free” is not a statistically supportable promise. The target is repeated, out-of-sample skill after accounting for uncertainty, market prices and selection bias. Proper forecast scoring and interval estimation help quantify evidence; they do not guarantee profit.

Current gate status: not run. No joined historical panel, timestamped odds archive, model forecasts or wagers are stored in the repository.
Each gate is a hard qualification requirement. Rows are proposed controls, not reported test results.
