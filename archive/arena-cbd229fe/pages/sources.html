Source register.

Primary football facts are linked to the NFL or NFL GSIS. Statistical guidance is linked to its publisher or standard-setting source. Every item below was opened or independently checked via official publisher search results on the research date.

Scope distinction: verified source availability is not the same as data ingestion or verification of every underlying record. No raw NFL dataset, odds history or model output is included in this repository.
Source claims are limited to the content stated on the linked page. Use the links for manual review and note each source's caveats.
