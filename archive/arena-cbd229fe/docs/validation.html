Correlation protocol

For binary legs A and B, report the empirical joint probability P(A∩B), the marginals, sample size, and uncertainty. If a correlation coefficient is additionally reported, define the exact variables and estimator; it is not interchangeable with a parlay payout or a conditional probability. Estimate only from matched, same-decision-time observations and validate on later data. Blank means not measured—not zero.

Leaderboard design

Show eligibility first (all gates passed); then display net return alongside sample size, calibration, uncertainty interval, maximum drawdown, and test window. Keep a frozen holdout untouched during selection. Require a minimum sample determined before testing; do not choose that threshold after seeing results. A top rank is not evidence of skill unless uncertainty and repeatability support it.
