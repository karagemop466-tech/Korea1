Strategy board

24 candidate signals, one leaderboard, and the gates that decide what counts.

How the board works

Every strategy is a fixed, published rule applied to 27,330 bets between 2006 and 2026 where both sides of the market were quoted. Ranking is tier first, then luck-adjusted z — so a strategy with a flattering ROI can never outrank a better-validated one. Tiers: A all 8 gates, B partial, C watchlist, F fails sample/integrity, X significantly negative.
