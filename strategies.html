Signal families and why they might matter

  • Model disagreement: a trade only has positive expected value if the model is better calibrated than the available price, not merely different from it.
  • Situational angles: rest, weather and recent form are hypotheses. Their availability timestamps and multiple-testing burden must be controlled.
  • Parlays: multiplying standalone prices does not establish an executable correlated ticket. Distinct games can share latent slate effects.
  • Player props: opportunity, role and game-script dependence matter, but no verified price history means no validated prop strategy.
  • Closing-line value: a useful prospective diagnostic only with genuine entry timestamps and comparable bookmaker closing quotes; it is not a guarantee of profit.
