How the competition is scored

Every strategy is staked one unit per bet at the recorded closing price — no line shopping, no assumed -110, no best-of-book. Pushes refund. A strategy is only ranked as skill if it clears all eight gates on the Validation page. Reward tiers are deliberately withheld from anything that has not cleared them.
