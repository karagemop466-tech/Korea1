Pricing protocol

For decimal odds D and a binary wager with no push or void, the break-even probability is 1/D and expected net return per unit is p × D − 1. For American odds, convert positive A to 1 + A/100 and negative A to 1 + 100/|A|. Pushes, voids, limits, fees and actual attainable prices require explicit settlement modeling. Market implied probability includes margin and is not automatically a fair probability.
