What is not satisfied, and why

Two items are not met and one is partial, and none of them is papered over: player-level statistics and prop prices are unreachable from this environment, so no prop-combination ROI is claimed (Player props); team-total prices are absent for the same reason; and the data snapshot is pinned rather than current, with the drift to upstream HEAD measured on the Verification page. Each is listed with what would unblock it on the Flagged for review page.
