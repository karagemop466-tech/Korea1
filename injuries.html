7 · Method notes and measured environment probes

Rules this page follows

  • A capture is only usable if it is timestamped before the kickoff of the game it describes. The 2026 week-10 capture was taken on 2026-11-12, before the 20:15 ET kickoff, and the report itself is the league's pre-game publication.
  • A club chart publishes an order; it does not publish a game-day role. This site therefore quotes the chart cells and does not call anyone a confirmed starter or replacement (Q30, Q32, S17).
  • Absence of a player from the report is not evidence that he is healthy, and presence is not evidence that he will play: clubs list only players with a report entry, and game status may be blank.
  • League-wide rates cannot be computed from these captures: 1 of 14 week-10 games is transcribed, and 1 of 16 for 2019 week 5 (the first game on that page).
