7 · Method notes and measured environment probes

Rules this page follows

  • A capture is only usable if it is timestamped before the kickoff of the game it describes. The 2026 week-10 capture was taken on 2026-11-12, before the 20:15 ET kickoff, and the report itself is the league's pre-game publication.
  • A club chart publishes an order; it does not publish a game-day role. This site therefore quotes the chart cells and does not call anyone a confirmed starter or replacement (Q30, Q32, S17).
  • Absence of a player from the report is not evidence that he is healthy, and presence is not evidence that he will play: clubs list only players with a report entry, and game status may be blank.
  • League-wide rates cannot be computed from these captures: 1 of 14 week-10 games is transcribed, and 1 of 16 for 2019 week 5 (the first game on that page).

Probes run on 2026-11-12

  • Shell egress to www.nfl.com — TLS handshake refused — the automated build cannot read NFL pages
  • Agent page-fetch tool to www.nfl.com — 200 for the injury hub, week pages, game centre, schedule, scores and roster pages
  • www.nfl.com/teams/<club>/depth-chart — 404 (verified on the Arizona Cardinals path)
  • www.nfl.com/teams/<club>/roster?tab=depth — 200 but the same alphabetical roster
  • www.nfl.com/scores/2026/REG8 — 502 on the first request; the canonical /scores/2026/week-8 returned 200

8 · What would move this from PARTIAL to MET

  1. League-wide depth with a timestamp. A capture of all 32 club charts (2 are verified) or official inactives, each dated before kickoff, with the 1st/2nd/3rd-string rule written down (S17, Q30, Q32).
  2. A timestamped weekly capture. Every report stored with a UTC timestamp and the kickoff time, so leak risk is measurable rather than assumed (S16, Q32).
  3. A gated deficit index. Starter-loss scoring per unit, computed only from pre-kickoff inputs, then run through all eight gates and compared with the market's own line (S18, Q34).
  4. Prices. Without Path B an availability signal still cannot be monetised, so it cannot earn a reward even if it predicts.
