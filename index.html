Decision brief

Start with singles and a probability forecast, then evaluate parlays and player combinations only when joint outcomes can be measured. A profitable-looking backtest alone is not evidence of skill. Luck cannot be eliminated; uncertainty, leakage, selection bias and dependence must be measured.
