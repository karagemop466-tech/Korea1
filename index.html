What the audit changed

  • Default builds now use the published commit, not a silently moving upstream branch.
  • Missing weather is no longer filled from the full history. Weather and roof fields are excluded from the default model until their pre-kickoff availability is verified.
  • Model and market errors are compared on identical games. Parlays use simultaneous kickoffs, not a whole week selected with hindsight.
  • Significance uses weekly blocks and Holm correction. The deflated Sharpe is provisional because the complete search history is unknown.
  • No strategy is called “skill” merely because it was profitable. No cash prize or capital allocation is authorized.
