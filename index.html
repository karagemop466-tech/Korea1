What this project does

It originates its own NFL lines from a market-free model, prices them, then tests a fixed library of betting strategies against the closing market. The point is not to produce a winner at any cost: it is to apply gates that remove luck, and to publish the result honestly — including when the result is that nothing survives.

Line origination

Three independent model families (weighted ridge, matchup points, Elo) are fitted walk-forward and averaged. The ensemble is the published line. Its accuracy is measured against the closing market on the same games.

Strategy competition

18 strategies — single bets, parlays and angle rules — are run over every priced game. Each must clear five statistical gates and three structural ones before it can be called skill rather than luck.

See the leaderboard →

Luck removal

Stationary bootstrap, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR and deflated Sharpe ratios, plus White's reality check across the whole library. The headline: the best strategy in the library is not distinguishable from luck.

Headline findings

  1. The market is still better than the model. Spread MAE 10.215 (market) vs 10.527 (ours); totals 10.527 vs 10.727. The gap is roughly a third of a point and it is the whole game.
  2. Nothing clears the gates. 0 of 18 strategies survive. The best raw ROI (+21.6%) sits on a 3-leg parlay ladder and dies the moment it is deflated for the number of trials.
  3. White's reality check does not reject. p = 0.144 across 18 strategies and 425 weekly blocks — the library's best performance is consistent with luck.
  4. The pricing environment is sharp. Mean two-way hold in the closing prices is 2.72% on spreads — a low-margin book, so the break-even win rate is about 51.4%, not 52.4%.
  5. Same-game correlation is structural, not cosmetic. Cover and moneyline on the same team correlate at φ = 0.676; cover and total correlate at φ = 0.024. Independent pricing of a same-game parlay is wrong for one pair and fine for the other.
  6. Prop legs are correlated, and the market cannot be checked. Player-level play-by-play is now ingested (2019–2022, 21,033 player-games) and prop lines are originated out of sample: AUC 0.66–0.73. Same-game legs are measurably dependent — QB passing yards with his own WR1's receiving yards lift the joint hit rate 1.14× over independence — but no reachable source carries a prop price, so every prop combo fails the economic gate and nothing is awarded.
