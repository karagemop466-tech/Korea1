Headline finding

Across 27,330 bets in 24 strategies, no strategy survives all eight validation gates on this data. The best raw performer (Wind >= 15 mph unders, +7.1% ROI over 430 bets) fails the false-discovery-rate gate, and White's Reality Check gives p = 0.970. The honest conclusion is that the closing market is efficient against public-data signals — and the lab is built to say so rather than to invent an edge.

What is still useful: a reproducible line-origination model (see Originated lines), measured correlation values for parlay pricing (Correlation), and a gate framework that will flag a real edge if one appears.

What the lab produced

1 · Originated lines

A walk-forward ridge power-rating model prices every game itself (spread and total) using only games played before kickoff. Against the market it is close but not better, which is the expected and honest result:

  • Spread MAE vs the result: 10.65 (market 10.24)
  • Total MAE vs the result: 11.27 (market 10.62)
  • Model beats the close on 45.4% of games

4 · Selective skill test

Each season the allocator funds only strategies that were credentialled on prior seasons. That is the honest test of "selective skill":

  • Selected portfolio ROI: -3.3%
  • Betting everything: -1.9%
  • Positive seasons: 5/18

Selection did not beat the naive portfolio, so the lab reports that instead of claiming a working meta-strategy.
