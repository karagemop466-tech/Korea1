Opening and closing numbers we actually hold — opens for 2011–2021, closes, freezes — each with its tier and its blanks' reasons.

What this page is. The archive-populated companion to price-snapshot.html, which keeps every opening and closing cell blank because no official NFL source publishes betting lines. This page shows the third-tier numbers the repository actually holds — each row labelled with its source tier, each blank carrying the reason it is blank.
