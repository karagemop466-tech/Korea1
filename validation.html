Validation

Gates, luck-removal statistics and the reward ladder that decides what gets published.

The eight gates

A strategy is only published as validated if it clears all eight. The gates are deliberately hard to pass: on this data set none do, which is the correct output for a market-efficient sport.

GateRequirement
G1_pricedEvery bet carries a real two-sided quote from the upstream file (no assumed −110).
G2_no_lookaheadFeatures and model output reproduce exactly when the future is deleted — enforced by tests/test_no_lookahead.py.
G3_sampleAt least 200 settled bets across 6+ seasons.
G4_significanceBenjamini-Hochberg FDR ≤ 0.1 across all strategies AND a positive luck-adjusted z.
G5_out_of_samplePositive ROI in both the train and test blocks with ≥50% retention.
G6_economicsPoint ROI > 0, bootstrap lower bound > −2%, and ≥90% probability the edge is positive.
G7_stability≥55% of seasons positive, no single season >40% of profits, worst leave-one-season-out ROI > −2%.
G8_diversificationAbsolute correlation with every other strategy < 0.8.

Tiers and rewards

The leaderboard is a competition with a disclosed points ladder rather than cash: tier determines status, then a 0–100 score (60 × P(edge>0) + 20 × out-of-sample retention + 15 × season hit rate + 5 × sample size). The score is a design choice, stated as such; the tier is what governs publication.

TierRequirementLabel on the board
AAll eight gates passValidated

Removing luck: the three tests that matter

1 · De-vigged expectation

Each bet's own two-way price implies a fair win probability. Comparing observed wins with the sum of those probabilities gives a z-score that is immune to "it just picked favourites". This is the primary skill test, and it is what the leaderboard sorts on.

2 · Block bootstrap

ROI confidence intervals resample whole weeks, so dependence between bets in the same week (weather, correlated outcomes) is respected. Naive per-bet bootstraps are too narrow.

3 · Multiplicity

Testing 24 strategies at once guarantees flattering outliers. Benjamini-Hochberg controls the false discovery rate at q = 0.1, and White's Reality Check tests the maximum statistic over the whole search.

Reality Check result

Best observed mean return 0.0927 per bet across 24 strategies; Reality Check p = 0.970 over 346 weekly observations. That is the probability of seeing a best-of-24 result this good by chance: the search is not finding an edge.

Portfolio verdict

Selected portfolio mean ROI -3.3% versus -1.9% for betting everything — selection added noise. Positive seasons 5 of 18. The rule and its failure are both published so the next session can test a better allocator against the same baseline.

What would change the conclusion

  • Verified historical prop prices (currently IR-03): props are the least efficient market available, and the correlation work here already quantifies the structure a prop strategy would need.
  • Injury and lineup data at scale: the single biggest missing input for any pre-game model.
  • Opening lines across all seasons: line movement is only testable for 2021 (IR-06).
