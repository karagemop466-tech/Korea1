A preregistered, chronological evaluation is required before a strategy can appear as eligible on the leaderboard.

No gate passed. Thresholds, reward budget and significance procedure must be preregistered; none are represented as validated. Results can remain inconclusive even after all data are collected.

Reducing luck, not removing it

Use proper probability scores, game-level resampling, confidence intervals and repeated forward tests. Correct for the number of strategies tried and disclose every trial, including failures. These steps reduce false discoveries; they cannot prove that luck is absent or guarantee future performance.
