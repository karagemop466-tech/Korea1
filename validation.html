Validation gates

Eight gates, applied to every strategy before any of them is allowed to claim skill.

The eight gates

GateQuestion it answersPass rule Source
G1 dataIs every bet settled against a real recorded price? 100% of bets carry a closing price from the pinned filegames.csv

Reality check across the whole library

White's (2000) reality check asks the question the leaderboard cannot: given that we tried 18 strategies, is the best one better than the best you would expect from pure luck? Resampling 425 weekly blocks 3,000 times gives a p-value of 0.186. It does not reject.

Why "not significant" is the interesting answer

At one unit per bet the standard deviation of a bet is about 1.0, so detecting a true edge of 1% per bet at 80% power needs roughly 78,000 bets — about 290 NFL seasons. Even a 5% edge needs about 3,100 bets, or twelve seasons. Any strategy published on a single season, or on a few hundred bets, is a coin flip with a story attached.

This is the core of the project's thesis: most betting 'edges' are under-powered noise. The gates are calibrated to say so rather than to flatter a result.
