Depth of the replacement

Who steps in when a starter cannot, how deep they come from, and how much depth is left behind them.

What this page is. The depth of the replacement, not only the fact of a replacement. For every slot whose first-listed player is unavailable, this page names the player who steps in, the depth string they come from (second string, third string, fourth string), and how many further players the club still has at that position. The depth chart is the club's own published chart; the availability is the official NFL injury report. The club-level depth impact, the cliff and the evidence state are published on the replacement-depth page.

A second-string replacement and a fourth-string replacement are different events. The first is a rotation; the second is a vacancy. The count of further players available at the same position is the number of times the club can absorb one more injury at that position before the position is exposed.
